First-time setup

  1. 1 · Camera & motion sensors

    Grants camera access and starts the gyroscope and accelerometer.

  2. 2 · Install Depth Anything 3

    Required for scanning. Downloads the ONNX model once (cached on this phone), builds the WebGPU session and checks its inputs. Then benchmarks it.

  3. 3 · Benchmark DA3

    Times the model at 280 → 504 px. The fastest size within the budget is selected; tap Use to override.

  4. 4 · Camera calibration

    Point at a textured scene, tap Calibrate, then rotate the phone in place (twist and tilt, don't walk) for 5 s. Solves FOV, camera↔IMU time offset and camera rotation.

  5. 5 · IMU rest check

    Lay the phone flat on a table, tap Measure and don't touch it for 3 s. Measures gyro bias (used at the start of every scan) and sensor noise.

  6. 6 · Scale test

    Scale of the walked path is the goal: walk a measured straight line (tape, floor tiles, ≥ 5 m) with the Walk scale test card and enter its true length. Each result records the DA3 input size used.
